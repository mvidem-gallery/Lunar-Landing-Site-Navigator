# Sky assets

- **Earth** — NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004, Reto Stöckli and Matthew R. Radcliff. Source: [SVS 12564](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/12564/) / [source image and public-domain attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:World.topo.2004-08.png). Resized and compressed.
- **Stars** — David Nash / Astronomy Nexus, [HYG Database v3.8](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/v3/hyg_v38.csv.gz), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The bundled subset `stars-hyg.bin` retains J2000 right ascension, declination and visual magnitude for stars of magnitude 7.3 or brighter, excluding the Sun. Fields are quantized as two little-endian 16-bit values (RA over 0–360°, declination over −90–90°) and one byte (magnitude × 22 after adding 2). This derived dataset is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
- **Lunar rotation** — [NAIF pck00011.tpc](https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/generic_kernels/pck/pck00011.tpc), IAU 2009 periodic orientation terms. Fixed J2000 star positions and this analytic frame are intended for sky visualization.
- **Earth orientation and lighting** — [JPL Horizons observer tables](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/manual.html), quantities 14, 15 and 32. The displayed map is a historical composite. Solar limb darkening is a simple display model.
